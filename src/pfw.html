Your first week on the account

If you learn nothing else here, learn this sequence. It is what separates an analyst who is trusted in month two from one who is still being checked in month six.

  1. Sit the Fundamentals certification first Before any of the below, work through Fullstory Learn and pass the Fundamental Product Certification. Unlimited attempts, 80% to pass, a few hours. It is canonical where this platform is merely careful, and it hands you a credential to put in front of anyone who asks what qualifies you.
  2. Get your own Fullstory account and break it Instrument a throwaway page. Fire events with wrong types. Mask something and watch the replay. Call setIdentity twice with different uids and see what happens. You cannot teach a tool you have only been shown.
  3. Learn one client's numbers before their tool Reg-to-FTD, deposit success, NGR per active, D7 retention. Walk in already knowing which of those is their problem.
  4. Run the error-click exercise on day one of capture Top five error signatures, sessions affected, downstream conversion gap. It is thirty minutes of work and it is the most reliable first win in this vertical.
  5. Pair every replay with a number, always Make it a personal rule from your first week and never break it. It is the single habit that most distinguishes a credible analyst.
  6. Say the compliance line before you are asked "I optimise for comprehension and speed, not completion, on regulated journeys." Say it in the kick-off. It changes how a gambling client treats you permanently.
  7. Find the BI lead and buy them a coffee They control the join between behaviour and money. Without them you have a UX tool; with them you have a revenue instrument.
  8. Write down the client-specific things nobody will tell you twice Which PSP, who owns the cashier, who is hostile, which release broke what. Use the notes box on every module in this platform — it saves across sessions.
Last

Your clients do not want session replay. They want to know why players who cost them £200 each to acquire are leaving before they deposit. Fullstory is how you find out. Never sell the tool; sell the answer.
