What is verified, and what is judgement

A reference you cannot audit is a reference you should not quote. This page states plainly which claims in this platform were checked against Fullstory's own documentation, which are reasoned from how the product works, and which are estimates you must not present as fact.

Date

Product facts below were checked against Fullstory's live help centre and developer documentation on 13 September 2026. Fullstory ships continuously. Before anything goes in front of a client, re-check the specific claim — and treat any statement here that is more than a quarter old as needing a look.

Three tiers of claim

Verified

Checked against Fullstory's own documentation on the date above. Safe to state to a client. Listed below with the source.

Reasoned

Follows from how the product demonstrably works — the nine build patterns, the capture model's consequences, the analytical method. Stable, because it does not depend on a menu label. Say it with confidence; expect to explain your reasoning.

Judgement

Consulting experience encoded as advice: onboarding sequencing, archetype risks, benchmark ranges, what to say in a room. Useful, arguable, and not to be presented as fact. Attribute it to yourself, not to a source.

Use it

Send a client the Fullstory link, not your slide, whenever the question is "is that actually true". Citing the vendor's own documentation ends a debate that citing yourself only prolongs — and the "Why do I see different numbers" article in particular will save you an afternoon with a sceptical BI team.

What is explicitly not verified

  • The specific UI steps inside the 153 build recipes. The patterns are reasoned from the data model and are stable. Any step naming a particular control, chart type or menu option was written from knowledge of the product, not from a screen. Check the current interface before you turn a recipe into a client-facing how-to guide.
  • Plan-dependent limits. Retention periods, session quotas, which roles exist, and which products are included all vary by contract. Read the client's own account, never this platform.
  • Every benchmark range. See the confidence tag on each KPI, and the explanation in the encyclopedia's header.
  • Regulatory detail. The regulatory module is oriented correctly and is not legal advice. Licence conditions change; the client's compliance team is the authority, and saying so out loud costs you nothing and buys you a great deal.
  • Vendor lists. The platform, PSP, KYC and CRM names in the systems module are representative of the market, not a verified current list of any client's stack.

Keeping it honest

When you find something in here that is wrong — and you will, because you will be using the product daily and this was written without it in front of me — fix it in the file rather than working around it. The two places that matter are data-kpi-build.js for recipes and the relevant module section in index.html. Then update the date at the top of this page so the next person knows how fresh it is.
